Techno-Economic Analysis of Energy Systems in Thakle Namuna Basti: A Case Study of Distributed Energy System Planning in Rural Nepal

Shah, M.; Koirala, K.; Upreti, S.; Sanjel, N.

IOP Conference Series: Materials Science and Engineering, Vol. 1279, No. 1, Article 012007 (2023)

View via DOI Download PDF

Abstract

This study presents a techno-economic analysis of distributed energy system planning for Thakle Namuna Basti, a rural community in Nepal. The work evaluates the technical feasibility and economic viability of renewable and distributed energy infrastructure to inform sustainable electrification strategies for off-grid and under-served communities.
